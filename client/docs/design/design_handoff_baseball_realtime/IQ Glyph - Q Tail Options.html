Baseball IQ glyph: diamond → Q

The idea: draw home plate as a real plate (open, point down, inside the base paths) and add a tail, so the brand diamond reads as a Q (question, IQ) while the logo stays a plain diamond. Each card shows the glyph large next to the logo, then at the real sizes it has to work at: the 19px header button (closed and open), the 14px panel title, 16px on the dark band, and 14px as a Home bullet.

What to look for. In the header row: does the button read as "ask a question" rather than a second logo? On the dark band: does the tail survive at 16px? For the horizontal tails: does the tail read as a Q's stroke, or as a baseline the diamond is sitting on? Tailed variants C–E stay in the 24px box; F nudges the diamond 1px up/left so the angled tail doesn’t make it sit optically high-left inside the 38px button.
